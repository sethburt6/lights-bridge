## Download

**Click `LightsBridge.dmg` under "Assets" below.** It works on any Mac running macOS 11 or newer, both Intel and Apple Silicon.

Permanent link to the newest version (good for a bookmark):
https://github.com/sethburt6/lights-bridge/releases/latest/download/LightsBridge.dmg

## Install (first time)

1. Open the downloaded **LightsBridge.dmg**.
2. In the window that appears, drag **Lights Bridge** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Eject the "Lights Bridge" disk (click the eject button next to it in Finder).
4. Open **Lights Bridge** from your Applications folder.
5. The Mac will say it can't check the app. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
6. A colored dot and the word "Lights" appear next to the clock. It now starts by itself every time the Mac logs in.

Then set up ProPresenter and the lighting board: [Full setup guide](https://github.com/sethburt6/lights-bridge/blob/main/docs/SETUP.md)

## Update (already installed)

1. Hold the **Option** key, click the Lights dot by the clock, and choose **Quit**.
2. Open the new **LightsBridge.dmg** and drag **Lights Bridge** onto **Applications**. Choose **Replace** when asked.
3. Open Lights Bridge from Applications again. Your settings are kept.

## Something not working?

See the one-page checklist: [Lights didn't change?](https://github.com/sethburt6/lights-bridge/blob/main/docs/VOLUNTEER.md)

You can always run the lights by hand on the board. Lights Bridge never changes anything stored on the board.
